AI-Enabled Portfolio Analysis Framework

Comprehensive Guide for Modern Investment Management in India

Step-by-Step Portfolio Analysis Process (India Context)

1
Define Mandate & Benchmark(s)
Investment Objective: Growth/Income/Absolute Return strategies
Constraints: Liquidity requirements, tax implications, ESG compliance
Appropriate Benchmarks: NIFTY 50, NIFTY 500, Midcap 150, custom blend based on portfolio composition
2
Data Ingestion & Normalization
Data Sources: CAS/RTAs (CAMS/KFintech), broker back office, DP, MF platforms (BSE Star MF/NSE NMF II)
Data Cleaning: Corporate actions (splits/bonus/dividends), sector mapping (GICS/AMFI)
Classification: Asset class, region, currency tagging
3
Cash-flow Reconstruction
Transaction Ledger: Build comprehensive transaction history
Separation: Distinguish contributions/withdrawals from P&L impact
4
Performance Measurement
TWRR: Time-weighted return (geometric linking) for manager skill assessment
MWRR/XIRR: Money-weighted return for client experience evaluation
Rolling Analysis: 1/3/5-year rolling periods and since inception performance
5
Risk Analysis
Volatility Metrics: Standard deviation, downside deviation, max drawdown
Market Risk: Beta, tracking error, correlation matrix
Advanced Risk: VaR/Expected Shortfall, liquidity analysis (ADVT/impact cost)
Concentration Risk: Top positions analysis, sector caps validation
6
Risk-Return Quality
Key Ratios: Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar ratios
Information Ratio: Active risk/return analysis
Capture Ratios: Upside/Downside market capture analysis
7
Style & Factor Exposure
Factor Analysis: Size, value, quality, momentum, low-volatility exposure
Style Drift: Monitor deviation from investment policy
Factor Models: Fama-French/Barra model implementation
8
Attribution Analysis
Brinson-Fachler: Allocation/selection/interaction effects by sector/cap
Attribution Methods: Holdings-based vs transaction-based attribution
9
Valuation & Fundamentals (Equity)
Valuation Metrics: Weighted P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, ROE/ROCE, FCF yield
Dividend Yield: Portfolio vs benchmark comparison
Dispersion Analysis: Tail risk assessment
10
Fixed-Income Analysis
Duration & Convexity: Interest rate sensitivity analysis
Credit Analysis: YTM, spread, credit buckets, issuer/sector concentration
Stress Testing: Parallel/twist shock scenarios
11
Costs & Tax Analysis
Cost Components: TER, brokerage, slippage analysis
Tax Efficiency: Realized/unrealized gains, tax-loss harvesting
Distribution Impact: Dividend distribution tax implications
12
Scenario & Stress Testing
Macro Scenarios: Interest rate +200 bps, FX, crude oil shocks
Historical Stress: 2008/2020-style market crash replay
Idiosyncratic Risk: Single-name crash, sector-specific shocks
13
Optimization & Rebalancing
Target Allocation: Minimize tracking error for desired alpha
Liquidity Awareness: Impact cost consideration in rebalancing
Post-tax Optimization: Tax-efficient rebalancing strategies
14
Governance & Compliance
Suitability Check: Alignment with client risk profile
Policy Monitoring: IPS breach identification and reporting
Regulatory Compliance: SEBI/DPDP documentation requirements
